Word limits are not enforced, but we encourage reviewers to keep
rebuttals brief and to focus on answering specific questions or
addressing specific misunderstandings, especially those that are shared
by multiple reviewers. In general, it's a good idea to make it easy for
reviewers to find the answers to their individual questions in the
rebuttal. The more you write, the less likely it is that reviewers will
pay attention to all of it.

The paper you submitted before the submission deadline is the paper
being evaluated by reviewers, so while additional information or data
may in some cases give reviewers a better understanding of your result,
a paper that relies on additional data submitted through the rebuttal in
order to be acceptable is likely to be considered not ready for
publication.

Finally, please remember that POPL follows a policy of double-blind
until acceptance for publication. That means your rebuttal should follow
the same guidelines for double-blind as the original submission.
