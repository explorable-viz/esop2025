\subsection{\thmref{semantics-eq}}
\label{app:proofs:semantics-eq}

Given that desugaring and the surface semantics are deterministic (\lemrefTwo{baseline:surface:eval:determinism}{baseline:desugar:determinism}), it is sufficient (but less verbose) to prove:
\begin{enumerate}
   \item If $\gamma, s \evalS v$ then $\exists v'.\;\gamma', e \evalS v'$ with $v \desugar v'$.
   \item If $\gamma', e \evalS v'$ then $\exists v.\;\gamma, s \evalS v$.
\end{enumerate}
