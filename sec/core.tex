\subsection{Continuations and eliminators}

A \emph{continuation} $\kappa$ is either a term or an \textit{eliminator}. Eliminators are trie-like
objects which can serve as a plausible desugaring target for surface-language pattern-matching constructs are desugared. An eliminator $\sigma$ is \textit{partial} if it is not defined for every element of its domain.
