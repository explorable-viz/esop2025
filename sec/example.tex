\section{Motivating Example}

We present an overview of \OurLang via a motivating example from data science.
Modern climate models such as CITE consist of a large number of interacting modules.
An important yet difficult task for engineers and scientists who work with or on
these models is interpreting the complex interactions between the various modules.

A common task performed in such models is the aggregation of data that may be
spatially or temporally distributed. For this section, we restrict our focus
to the aggregation of two time-series. The problem involves taking two disparate
time-indexed series of data, and combining them together into a single unified
series. This is complicated by the fact that the 2 series may not be of the same
length, may not necessarily be scaled in the same way, and the possibility that
the operations we perform to fuse points from one series to another can be itself
a non-trivial operation.

Methods are still proposed for this problem today, but we shall illustrate our
approach using the classic solution of Dynamic Time Warping.
