- prior work on resugaring (Pombio/Krishnamurthi, 2014)
- prior work on lazy desugaring (Yang et al, FLOPS 2022)
  - basic idea:
    - treat surface language and core language as a single (staged?) language
    - delay desugaring until necessary to take a core step
    - from core context rules and desugaring rules, derive for combined language:
      - context rules, e.g. C ::= (And C e) | (Or C e) | ..
      - desugaring rules, e.g. (And v e) -> (if v e #f)
      - filtering rules, e.g. to exclude (if e1 e2 e3) from evaluation sequence
    - properties: extensibility, emulation, laziness
  - formalisation not particularly nice
    - s-expressions
    - metavariable confusion

- observation about desugaring rules in POPL 2022 paper
  - all rules are of the form C_s(p1, .., pn) -> C_c(e1, .. en)
  - fwd: annotations on surface term conjoined to create to annotation on core term
  - bwd: annotations on core terms disjoined to create annotation on surface term
  - can be automatically derived given datatype-generic description of languages
  - GC generated automatically e.g. for user-defined sugar or custom views
  - fit well with approach taken in Yang et al 2022
